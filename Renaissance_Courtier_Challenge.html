The Renaissance Courtier Challenge

Chapter 8, Day 1 • Read pages 68–72 first • About 7 minutes

Your mission

Castiglione wrote about how a courtier could earn a ruler's trust and influence decisions. Investigate what made a courtier effective—and whether appearing admirable is the same as being admirable.

Historical thinking: Castiglione's recommendations are evidence of an ideal promoted by his book, not proof that every Renaissance courtier behaved this way.

Investigation complete

Castiglione's courtier needed real skills, but also knew how to be noticed. Those qualities could help someone advise a ruler, yet a polished appearance does not prove honesty or wisdom.

Remember: Proverbs 22:1 values a good name above riches. A genuinely good reputation grows from character, not just performance.
